Overview

Welcome! In this experiment, you will participate in a sequence of interactive games. Your goal is to make choices that maximize your total profit based on the rules of each game.


  • You will play {{NUM_GAMES}} different games.
  • Each game lasts for {{ROUNDS_PER_GAME}} rounds (for a total of {{NUM_ROUNDS}} rounds).
  • In every single round, you will be randomly matched with a new participant in the room.
Your Decision (Each Round)
  • In each round, you and your randomly assigned opponent will choose between two actions:
  • Action A
  • Action B

You have a maximum of 1 minute to make your choice per round. If the timer expires, a choice will be randomly made for you.


How Your Profit Is Determined

Before making your choice, you will be shown a Payoff Matrix. Your profit in a round depends simultaneously on:

  • Your choice (A or B)
  • Your opponent's choice (A or B)

The matrix will show exactly how many points you and your opponent will earn for every possible combination of choices. You will act either as the "Row" player or the "Column" player.

Sequence of Each Round
  1. Decision Phase: View the matrix and choose A or B privately.
  2. Results Phase: Discover your opponent's choice and see your profit for that round.

Note: After every 5 rounds, there will be a brief pause to review class-level statistics for the game you just completed.
