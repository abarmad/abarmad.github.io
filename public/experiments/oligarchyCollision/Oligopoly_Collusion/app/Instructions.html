Overview

You will participate in a computerized market experiment with {{numPlayers}} participants. In each round, you act as a firm choosing how much quantity to produce. Your goal is to maximize your total profit. You will interact for {{numRounds}} rounds.

Your Decision (Each Round)
  • On your screen, you will choose one option:
  • Low Quantity (L)
  • High Quantity (H)

You have to submit your choice before the timer expires.


How Your Profit Is Determined

Your profit depends on:

  • Your own choice (Low or High)
  • The number of other participants who chose low

After each round, your screen will display the number of low and number of high quantity and your profit for that round.

Timing and Rounds

After all submissions:

  • The experiment consists of multiple rounds.
  • Each round has a fixed time limit (shown on your screen).
  • If you do not submit a choice in time, a default option may be assigned.
Feedback After Each Round

You will see:

  • Your choice
  • The number of other participants who chose Low and High
  • Your profit for the round
  • Your cumulative profit so far
Matching
  • You will remain in the same group of participants for all rounds (unless stated otherwise).
  • All decisions are anonymous.
Payment
  • Your final payoff is based on your total profit across all rounds.
  • This may be converted into a monetary reward according to the rules provided by the instructor.
Rules
  • No communication with other participants unless explicitly allowed.
  • Make your decisions independently.
  • Do not attempt to identify or signal to others.
Important
  • Your earnings depend on both your decisions and the decisions of others.
  • Try to make the best decision in each round to maximize your total payoff.
